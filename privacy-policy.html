Pixel Fins Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Pixel Fins is developed by Cade O'Brien. This policy explains how information is handled when you play Pixel Fins and use its optional services.

Local Game Data

The game saves aquarium progress, fish collections and names, inventory, settings, quest progress, and ad-reward records on your device. These saves support normal gameplay and offline play. The current version does not offer player accounts, cloud saves, or public leaderboards. Fish names and full aquarium saves are not included in the game's custom analytics events.

Optional Gameplay Analytics

Gameplay analytics is off by default. If you turn it on under Settings → Privacy, Unity Analytics processes device and session information and gameplay events such as tutorial progress, completed quests, catches, fishing failures, breeding, and earned ad rewards. Event details use game-content identifiers rather than text you enter. We use this information to understand how the game is played and improve its features and balance.

You can turn gameplay analytics off at any time in the same menu. “Delete Analytics Data” requests deletion through Unity Analytics without deleting your aquarium saves. The request requires an internet connection; follow the game's confirmation or retry message. Turning analytics off does not by itself delete previously collected information.

Optional Rewarded Advertising

Where enabled in your version, you can choose to allow optional rewarded ads and watch an ad for Fish Flakes. The game uses Unity LevelPlay with ironSource Ads and Unity Ads. You can play without enabling these ads. There are no forced interstitial or banner ads in the current integration.

Advertising services may process IP addresses, device and app information, identifiers where permitted, approximate location inferred from an IP address, and ad interactions or performance information to deliver and measure ads, limit repetition, and prevent fraud. Clicking an ad may take you to a third-party app or website governed by its own privacy policy.

The current game integration requests non-personalised advertising and sends a do-not-sell signal to supported networks before initialising LevelPlay. These settings do not make ads anonymous or prevent all processing by advertising services. The game does not request Apple's permission for cross-app tracking in the current integration.

Optional rewarded ads are off by default. You can change your preference under Settings → Privacy. Turning the preference off prevents new ad requests by the game; it does not delete data already processed by an advertising provider. A loaded or already-started ad and its reward callbacks may finish after a preference change.

Service Providers and Sharing

For optional ads on iOS, Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) checks whether a regional privacy message is required and stores the choices made through that message. Google may process technical information such as your IP address, device/app information, and consent status to provide this service. See Google's Privacy Policy. Where UMP requires a privacy-options entry point, Settings → Privacy includes “Ad Privacy Choices” to review or withdraw those choices. The game waits for UMP to permit ad requests in addition to your optional-ad preference. Google UMP is used for consent; ads continue to be served through Unity LevelPlay.

Unity provides the analytics and advertising services described above. Information needed for these services is processed under Unity's policies, including its Game Player and App User Privacy Policy and ironSource Privacy Policy. These policies describe provider processing, retention, international transfers, and privacy choices. The developer does not directly sell personal information.

Support Enquiries

If you email us, we receive the email address and information you choose to include, and use them to respond to your enquiry or privacy request. Please avoid sending passwords or other unnecessary sensitive information.

This Policy Website

This page is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information, including IP addresses, when you visit it, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. This page does not add advertising or analytics scripts.

Storage, Retention, and Security

Local saves remain on your device until removed using available save controls or the app is deleted. Copies may remain in device or operating-system backups according to your backup settings. Keep a backup of anything you wish to retain before deleting it.

Unity and its advertising services may process information outside your country, as explained in their policies. Provider retention and deletion processes are described in those policies. We retain support correspondence as needed to handle your enquiry and any applicable record-keeping obligations. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your Privacy Choices and Rights

You can play offline and leave analytics and optional ads disabled. To request access, correction, or deletion of information handled by the developer, or to raise a privacy concern, email us using the contact details below. Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to restrict or object to processing, receive a portable copy of information, withdraw consent, or complain to your local data-protection authority. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing already carried out.

For information held by Unity or its advertising services, use the in-game analytics deletion control where applicable and the privacy request options in the provider policies linked above. We may need enough information to identify and verify your request; please do not send unnecessary identity documents.

Children's Privacy

Pixel Fins is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children where parental consent is required. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can investigate and arrange deletion where appropriate.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when the game's features or services change. The date above identifies the latest revision. Material changes that require a new choice will be presented through the relevant consent flow before the affected optional processing begins.
