Report a problem

Please include your device model, iOS version, Pixel Fins version, what you were doing, and the steps that reproduce the problem. A screenshot can help. Do not send passwords, payment details or other sensitive information.

Saved progress

Progress is stored on your device. If a save appears to be missing, contact support before deleting or reinstalling the app, which may remove local progress. We cannot promise to recover a lost local save.

Accounts and cloud worlds

In versions with account features, tap your collection level at the top left during play, or Profile on the main menu, then Account & Cloud Saves. Unity sign-in is optional. Account worlds and device-only worlds use separate slots; copying device worlds into empty account slots is a separate choice.

Use Sync Worlds Now while online before moving to another device. If world versions differ, review them and choose which to keep. Downloaded account worlds remain playable offline. Unsynced changes can still be lost if local storage is removed. Signing out keeps account worlds separately cached and returns to the original device-only worlds.

Delete Pixel Fins Account removes this game's cloud account and downloaded account worlds on this device; it does not delete your Unity Player Account or original device-only worlds. If deletion is interrupted, reconnect and retry it from the account controls. Analytics deletion is separate under Privacy.

Optional services and privacy

Open Settings, then Privacy, to manage optional analytics and rewarded ads. When regional ad privacy options are required, Ad Privacy Choices is available there. You can play without enabling these optional services.

For a privacy question or request, contact the same email address. See the Pixel Fins Privacy Policy for details.
